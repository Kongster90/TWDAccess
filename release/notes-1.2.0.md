TWD Access makes The Walking Dead: The Telltale Definitive Series playable by ear, through your screen reader (NVDA, JAWS and others) or Windows' own voice.

## What's new in 1.2.0: the game's other languages

The game can show its text in French, German, Spanish, Portuguese, Italian, Russian, Simplified Chinese and Traditional Chinese as well as English. The mod now works much better in them:

- **Season One and 400 Days:** Enter does the right action in any language. Before, in French, Enter on the Motor Inn's truck looked at it instead of moving to it. The same goes for the "Again! Enter!" call during a fight, for talking, for which walker to deal with first, and for knowing which things are doors, gates and ladders.
- **Season One and 400 Days:** things the game names alike are numbered, "Porte 1" and "Porte 2", instead of getting English words mixed into their names.
- **Every season:** words written in capitals are read normally when they have accents or are in Russian, for example "Paramètres" and "Épisode 1" in French, "Выбор Сезона" in Russian. Before, they came out as "ParamÈTres" and "ÉPisode".
- **Chinese:** the mod no longer adds a full stop after the game's own.

Tested in Season One in French, Russian and Simplified Chinese, and in Season Two in French (dialog choices, names, Tab and Enter). The Final Season's menus were checked in French. English plays as before.

To change the language, open the game's main menu, then Settings, then Languages. The mod's own words, such as "Main menu" and "Tab to choose", are still in English. Use a screen reader voice that speaks the language you choose.

The zip now has `CHANGES.txt`, which lists what's new in each version.

## Install or update

1. Download `TWDAccess-1.2.0.zip` and unzip it anywhere. To update, unzip it over your old TWDAccess folder. Your settings are kept, since they live in the game folder.
2. Run `TWDAccess.exe` each time you play. It installs the mod into the game folder and starts the game through Steam.
3. On the title screen, press Enter.

You don't need to install anything else: .NET is built in. `README.txt` in the zip lists the keys and settings, and how to remove the mod.

## Requirements

- Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit.
- The game on Steam.
