TWD Access: what changed in each version
Newest first.


1.3.0 (3 November 2026): knowing what is in reach

Season One and 400 Days, where you walk about:

- Tab now says why you cannot use something from where you stand. "Out of reach": Enter walks
  you there. "Blocked": a walk there already stopped short, for example at a closed gate; such
  things come last in the list until you use something or walk well away. "In another area": the
  game will not let you use it until you have gone over there, for example through a gate in a
  fence. F6 says "blocked" and "in another area" too.
- After you open a door that leaves nothing to use beyond it, the mod says it is open, and Tab
  offers "Open doorway": Enter walks you through. In Episode 1 this is the Door to Pharmacy in
  the drugstore's office, where walking through starts the next scene. Before, nothing said to
  walk on.
- Episode 2, the dairy: the gate in the picket fence takes you up onto the farmhouse porch, and
  the way back down had nothing to Tab to. Tab now offers "Porch steps" while you are up there.

Tested in Season One: the pharmacy door, and the dairy's yard, porch and barn side.


1.2.0 (3 November 2026): the game's other languages

The game can be played in French, German, Spanish, Portuguese, Italian, Russian, Simplified
Chinese and Traditional Chinese as well as English. The mod now works much better in them:

- Season One and 400 Days: Enter does the right action in any language. Before, in French,
  Enter on the Motor Inn's truck looked at it instead of moving to it. The same goes for the
  "Again! Enter!" call during a fight, for talking, for which walker to deal with first, and for
  knowing which things are doors, gates and ladders.
- Season One and 400 Days: things the game names alike are numbered, "Porte 1" and "Porte 2"
  (door 1, door 2), instead of getting English words mixed into their names.
- Every season: words written in capitals are read normally when they have accents or are in
  Russian, for example "Paramètres" and "Épisode 1" in French, "Выбор Сезона" in Russian.
  Before, they came out as "ParamÈTres" and "ÉPisode".
- In Chinese, the mod no longer adds a full stop after the game's own.

Tested in Season One in French, Russian and Simplified Chinese, and in Season Two in French
(dialog choices, names, Tab and Enter). The Final Season's menus were checked in French. The
later seasons already did the right action on Enter in any language. English plays as before.

Still in English: the mod's own words, such as "Main menu", "Tab to choose" and "Say nothing".


1.1.0 (3 November 2026): the streaming setting

- A new setting, streaming, for streaming or recording with OBS. With streaming = true in the
  game folder's TWDAccess\settings.txt, TWD Access opens in a window of its own, called
  "TWD Access", so OBS's Application Audio Capture can record its beeps and tones: pick the
  window marked [TWDAccess.exe]. It is off by default and takes effect the next time
  TWDAccess.exe starts.


1.0.0 (31 October 2026): the first release

- Every season in the Definitive Series: Season One with 400 Days, Season Two, A New Frontier,
  The Final Season and Michonne, each played through from start to end with the mod, by ear.
- Menus, settings, save screens and each episode's choices screen are read in every season.
- Dialog choices are read as a numbered list: press 1 to 4. Timed choices wait for you.
- Tab and Shift+Tab move between the things you can use and the places you can walk to; Enter
  uses one, walking you there first if needed.
- Quick-time events, struggles, fights, guided paths, drags, hiding and leaning out: the mod
  says which key to press, and when.
- Aiming by a tone with the arrow keys: the shotgun and rifle scope in Season One, the bow in
  The Final Season.
- Action scenes at normal speed, in slow motion, or played for you (F11).
