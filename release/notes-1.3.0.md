TWD Access makes The Walking Dead: The Telltale Definitive Series playable by ear, through your screen reader (NVDA, JAWS and others) or Windows' own voice.

## What's new in 1.3.0: knowing what is in reach

In Season One and 400 Days, where you walk about:

- **Tab says why you can't use something from where you stand.**
  - "Out of reach": Enter walks you there.
  - "Blocked": a walk there already stopped short, for example at a closed gate. Such things come last in the list until you use something or walk well away.
  - "In another area": the game won't let you use it until you've gone over there, for example through a gate in a fence.

  F6 says "blocked" and "in another area" too.
- **Open doorways.** After you open a door that leaves nothing to use beyond it, the mod says it's open, and Tab offers "Open doorway": Enter walks you through. In Episode 1 this is the Door to Pharmacy in the drugstore's office, where walking through starts the next scene. Before, nothing said to walk on.
- **The dairy porch (Episode 2).** The gate in the picket fence takes you up onto the farmhouse porch, and the way back down had nothing to Tab to. Tab now offers "Porch steps" while you're up there.

Tested in Season One: the pharmacy door, and the dairy's yard, porch and barn side.

## Install or update

1. Download `TWDAccess-1.3.0.zip` and unzip it anywhere. To update, unzip it over your old TWDAccess folder. Your settings are kept, since they live in the game folder.
2. Run `TWDAccess.exe` each time you play. It installs the mod into the game folder and starts the game through Steam.
3. On the title screen, press Enter.

You don't need to install anything else: .NET is built in. `README.txt` in the zip lists the keys and settings, and how to remove the mod. `CHANGES.txt` lists what's new in each version.

## Requirements

- Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit.
- The game on Steam.
