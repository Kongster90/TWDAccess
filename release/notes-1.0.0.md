TWD Access makes The Walking Dead: The Telltale Definitive Series playable by ear, through your screen reader (NVDA, JAWS and others) or Windows' own voice.

## What's in this first release

- Every season in the Definitive Series: Season One with 400 Days, Season Two, A New Frontier, The Final Season and Michonne. Each was played through from start to end with the mod, by ear.
- Menus, settings, save screens and each episode's choices screen are read in every season.
- Dialog choices are read as a numbered list. Press 1 to 4 to pick one, and timed choices wait for you.
- Tab and Shift+Tab move between the things you can use and the places you can walk to. Enter uses the thing, and the mod walks you there first if needed.
- Quick-time events, struggles, fights, guided paths, drags, hiding and leaning out are covered: the mod says which key to press, and when.
- When you aim, the arrow keys move your aim and a tone guides you. This covers the shotgun and rifle scope in Season One and the bow in The Final Season.
- An action-scene setting, toggled with F11: normal speed, slow motion, or the mod plays the scene for you.

## Install

1. Download `TWDAccess-1.0.0.zip` and unzip it anywhere.
2. Run `TWDAccess.exe` each time you play. It installs the mod into the game folder and starts the game through Steam.
3. On the title screen, press Enter.

You don't need to install anything else: .NET is built in. `README.txt` in the zip lists the keys and settings, and how to remove the mod.

## Requirements

- Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit.
- The game on Steam.
